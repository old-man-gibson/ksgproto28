Mutagen Review

2026-10-23 · design review, nothing built · team run: three brainstormers, one design antagonist, rulings by the manager · source of record docs/MUTAGENS_REVIEW.md on branch docs/mutagens-review

The askMake mutagens more visually appealing and more rewarding to master past their current scope; keep the identity while making it distinct; make mutagens a bottom-up system that writes a character's base stats and attributes.
State todayThe engine is complete and well tested. The system is shut in the alpha with nothing to open it; its drops leak past the shutter and burn their discovery XP; one gene source spawns live; it writes no attributes (bare writes 0); its mastery pays a skill level and shows nothing else.
Identity keptTopology: you place things, and order and adjacency fall out of the placement. Nothing proposed adds an order field, a duration, a pair table or an enemy-keyed effect.
Bottom-upThe gene grants G = 8 × coefficient attribute points, split by its lean. Each attribute belongs to one door; an arm expresses its attributes at 50% unfed and 100% fed. Points enter the base attribute layer, under gear, level and skills (D).
MasteryFigures: twelve named graph patterns, each stamped once per run for discovery XP and +1.5 capacity (E). Later, monsters show their genome in the bestiary and a Copy button lays it out (Anatomy).
LooksCode-drawn pixel plates (shape = leg, hue = element, pips = grade); edges as 3 μm beads; per-route gauges at each door; a picker that previews before you place; a crest that carries the body to the sidebar and the stage; a specimen book (A–C, F, G).
AntagonistFix first: no terminal state, arithmetic holds; eight objections, all accepted (rulings below). The biggest: the Vivarium find could not fire, and discoveries pay nobody until Geneshaping manifests, which harvesting never does.
Your callEight questions, each with a default (open questions below). Nothing is built.

D. The body layer: the gene writes your attributes

round 1 · system

Rule: the expressed gene grants G = 8 × its coefficient attribute points, split by the gene's lean. Each attribute belongs to one door, the same assignment bare already uses (Str and Dex: Attacks; Con: Defenses; Int and Wis: Active; Cha: Special). An arm expresses its attributes at 50% unfed and 100% once the routes into it carry one per-route cap of graded weight. Routing into one arm never moves another arm's attributes. Points enter the base attribute layer, so the sidebar, attribute checks and every formula see them.

E. Figures: patterns the graph pays for

round 2 · system

A closed list of named patterns, each a test over the resolved chromosome. The first time your own chromosome meets one in a run, it is stamped: discovery XP, and +1.5 capacity for the rest of the run (Stability, capped at +18). The test text is always shown; the example build appears once stamped. Placement is free, so the reward is for finding a pattern once, never for holding it. Antagonist objections and rulings

design antagonist · verdict: fix first

No reachable terminal state; the arithmetic holds; Law 2 intact; no other discipline's identity duplicated. Eight objections, ranked:

#ObjectionRuling
1The Vivarium has no explore activity, and finds only fire from exploring. A caged hunt never advances explored, so the find never fires and the tab stays shut.Accepted. The Vivarium gets exploreTicks and an explore activity carrying the find; one guide line names the Vivarium.
2Discoveries pay nobody until Geneshaping manifests, and harvesting never manifests it, lift or no lift. Ran 20 seeds × 300 kills: 5 discoveries each, 0 XP.Accepted; it corrects F2's cause. harvestKill calls checkDiscovery for geneshaping before rolling, and the lift find grants Geneshaping XP. The test asserts XP, not known.
3Normalising shares across six attributes couples the arms: four Wards on the ring cut a slime king's mind attributes from 4.13 to 0.71 each. §4: arms are coupled by content, never by the engine.Accepted, different fix. No normalisation: each attribute belongs to one door (bare's own mapping), and an arm only sets its own attributes' expression (50% to 100%). Routing into one arm never moves another. No §4 ruling needed.
4The Genesis boon is read in collectBaseMods but not keyed in modFingerprint; buying it mid-run leaves the derived stats stale.Accepted. Genesis rank joins the T token.
5A figures:found prestige source pays only the player who picked mutagens; every other build harvests less for an equal climb.Accepted. Dropped; figures reach prestige through Geneshaping levels and discovery XP.
6After Allotment every gene writes attributes, which inverts §8's "attributes = bare's generalist channel" and the comment on bare.Accepted. §8 and the bare comment are rewritten in the same change.
7A lean summing to 0 divides by zero and NaN spreads through the stat block.Accepted. Lint in both validation layers (Σ = 1 within ε, every entry ≥ 0) and a 1/6 fallback.
8The order shipped the body layer into a shut system, and mastery was mostly deferred.Accepted. The lift goes first; expression (the graph's part of the body layer) ships with slice 1, so the body is a mastery lever from the start.

Open questions

your call

Each has a default, used until you answer.

  1. Body layer shape. Default: Allotment with expression (D). Alternative: Slope, where the body redistributes the +1-per-level gain instead of adding points (bare stays identical; a slime body loses about 0.9 points a level to clamping). Or both.
  2. Body budget. G = 8 × coefficient: Slime Fair 9, Fire Slime Fine 10, Slime King Divine 24.8, Surtr Divine 44.8. Default: these, then tuned with npm run pacing.
  3. Genesis boon. Default: yes, +6 bare points per rank, always fully expressed, costs 2/3/4/5/6. At rank 1 it equals Head Start for a player with no graph.
  4. Figures pay capacity. Default: +1.5 per figure, cap +18, resets with the run. Alternative: discovery XP only.
  5. Vivarium stock. Default: three shelved species in a caged hunt with no fight limit: Fire Slime (fire; also the Fire Slime gene), Ice Slime (ice), Slime Spider (toxic). Alternative: new species.
  6. Drops while shut. Default: none until the lift. Alternative: bank silently, no log lines.
  7. The capacity curve (F8). Default: leave §12 open; uniques are catalogue capstones, not bodies. Alternative: lower TIER_COST_EXPONENT from 1.35.
  8. Round 1 scope. Default: fixes, lift, body layer, diagram (steps 0–3).

Weighed and dropped

  • Training-XP multipliers per attribute: worth 6.2 × ln(m) trained levels (×2 = +4.3); nearly invisible.
  • Attribute caps from the body: flatten growth and strand attribute-gated content.
  • Global share normalisation across six attributes: couples the arms (objection 3).
  • A wear-time skill per genus discounting gene cost: no topology, and no gene supply to wear.
  • A prestige source for figures (objection 5).
  • A catalogue keyed on arrangements: placement is free, so an unbounded key is farmable; figures are the finite key.
  • Animated flow particles, a force-directed graph, a "best layout" button, per-mutagen bespoke icons (109 entries, no art budget), a chromosome mastery % bar.
  • Sever (cutting the enemy's edges in a fight) and two-centromere chimeras: enemy-keyed or undefined.

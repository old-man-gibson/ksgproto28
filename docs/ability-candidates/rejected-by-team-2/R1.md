### Terraform

`FLD-19` · Things on the battlefield · withdrawn by team 1 after the first review · appeal 3/5

Each spot keeps 100 points of paint split across damage types (fire, cold, shock, poison, slash, pierce, blunt, as the game defines them). Each hit you land moves 2 points toward its type, taken evenly from the others, and paint fades 5 points per in-game day toward an even split. The top type, if it holds at least 40 points, gives that type +0.5% damage per point (+50% at 100). At 70 points or more the ground acts: fire burns enemies for 1% max HP per second, cold slows them 15%, shock stuns 1 hit in 10, poison halves their healing, blunt cuts their armor 20%, slash and pierce bleed them for 0.5% max HP per second. Notes: Wild. A one-type build lights up a spot after about 35 hits; a mixed build gets nothing and is pushed to specialise. A player idling on one spot overnight finds it fully painted. ### Plague Chain

`FLD-06` · Things on the battlefield · failed both reviews · appeal 3/5

A poison cloud jumps to the next enemy and grows each jump.

Your first hit in a fight seeds a poison cloud on that enemy, 2% of its max HP per second at stage 1. When the host dies the cloud jumps to the nearest living enemy, back row included, one stage higher (each stage adds +50% of the base, cap 4x); against a lone enemy it never jumps and deals at most 1% per second. If the last host of a fight dies at stage 3 or higher, the next fight's first enemy of the same species starts infected at that stage minus 1 and the chain gains a link; the chain ends on a different species, a last host dying below stage 3, or a knockout. Each link adds +10% to all cloud damage (cap 15 links, +150%) and costs you 0.1% of max HP per second (cap 1.5%), so holding the chain trades a rising bleed for a rising cloud. Bosses and elites start at stage 1, take at most stage 2, and ignore the link bonus.

Notes: Medium. Example: 250 max HP, goblin camp packs of four. Link 5 bleeds 0.5% (1.25 HP a second) and gives +50% cloud damage; link 15 bleeds 1.5% (3.75 HP a second) and gives +150%, so a stage-4 cloud is 5% x 2.5 = 12.5% of max HP a second on every enemy of the pack. The decision is how long to stay on one species against your regeneration, and poison resistance cuts the bleed. Poison-immune enemies take nothing but still pass the cloud on. Differs from Fuse (ENM-19), an explosion on death; this is a carried damage-over-time with a cross-fight counter. Open: lone-enemy floors end the chain by design.

Plays: passive · Reads: formation rows, poison resistance, kill events, species of the next spawn

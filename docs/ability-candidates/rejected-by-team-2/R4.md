### Fitted Form

`R4-BDY-15` · Body and form · failed both reviews · appeal 3/5

Your form counters the enemy you face and re-fits when it dies.

Does: Your form re-fits at fight start and whenever your target dies. Wisp gives 20 dodge points. Breaker ignores 30% of armour. Hunter ignores 30% of dodge.
Costs: Wisp deals 25% less damage. Breaker costs 10 dodge points. Hunter cuts max HP by 15%.
Limit: Wisp if the target's hit takes 15% or more of your max HP. Else Breaker if its armour stops more damage than its dodge does. Else Hunter.
Grows: Each 50 kills made in a form raise its bonus by 1%, up to 50% more.
Fails: In a mixed group the form fits only your current target. The others meet the wrong body.

Notes: Material tags and the Wisp placeholder are gone. The three tests use numbers the engine already has. Worked example at level 10 with 200 max HP and a 40 hit: a wolf has dodge that stops 35% of your swings, armour that stops 5%, and a hit of 10 (5% of max HP). It makes you Hunter, so its effective dodge is 24.5%. A boar hits for 50 (25% of max HP). It makes you Wisp. A skeleton has armour that stops 40% and dodge 10%. It makes you Breaker, so its armour stops 28%. Ties go Wisp, then Breaker, then Hunter. At the 50% growth cap Breaker ignores 45% of armour. The 15% line is a tuning knob. Open check, unverified: the first six floors should give at least two different forms per floor. A lint over the bestiary can assert this, and if it fails the 15% line moves. Forms re-fit on target death only, not on every swing, so there is no flicker. A boss alone keeps one form for the fight. Differs from Wild Shape (ground picks the form), Reflex Stances (last event picks) and Invert and Declared Weakness (elements).

Plays: passive · Tier: experimental · InfiniteGacha: both games · Reads: enemy hit damage against your max HP, enemy armour, enemy dodge, kills made in each form

What the rework changed: Obj 1 (Wisp is a placeholder): there is no placeholder. Every form has a bonus and a cost, and the form is picked from enemy stats instead of a material tag. Ghosts need no special case.
Obj 2 (form rarely changes): the form follows hit size, armour and dodge, which differ between enemies on one floor. It re-fits whenever the target dies, as the review suggested. A wolf pack and a boar already give different forms on early floors. The floors 1-6 spread is a stated lint check, not an assumption.

### Tape

`R4-TMP-17` · Fight length and pacing · failed both reviews · appeal 2/5

Replay your fastest win against a species as the opener, at 150% bar speed.

Does: The fastest win against each species is recorded. The next fight against it opens by replaying the first 6 actions at 150% bar speed.
Costs: Rotation conditions are ignored while the tape plays. The replay stops when your HP is below 50%.
Grows: One more action replays per 5 wins against that species, up to 12. A faster win replaces the recording.
Fails: A species with no recording is fought by your rotation. An action you cannot pay for or no longer have on the bar is skipped.
Limit: At 100 wins in total pick Loop or Free Hand. Loop repeats the tape while the enemy is above 50% HP. Free Hand makes replayed actions cost no mana.

Notes: Save version changes: up to 12 action ids per species. Pack fights record and replay under the first enemy's species. Only wins are recorded. The replay now has an upside the rotation cannot produce, which is the 150% bar speed on the opener. The rotation still decides everything after the tape ends. Example: the recording is Fire, Fire, Slash, Fire, Slash, Fire. The six actions take 8 s instead of 12 s, and the player enters at 30% HP, so the replay never starts and the rotation can heal. The heal problem from round 1 is handled by the 50% HP stop, so ignoring conditions only costs flask and cleanse conditions on non-HP triggers. Honest weakness: a faster win replaces the recording, so the tape tends to match the current rotation. The speed is then the real gain and the recording mostly fixes which actions and in what order. Open option, not in the text: let the tape play actions that are no longer on the bar, which makes it a second, past bar and rewards swapping bars between fights. Differs from Opener, which is a flat front-loaded bonus with no recording. Differs from Learn By Hit and the rejected Metronome: nothing is scored.

Plays: passive · Tier: experimental · InfiniteGacha: IG needs: ordered skill use it can record, and an action speed stat · Reads: action log per fight, kill time, species, HP fraction, bar speed

What the rework changed: Obj 1 (the replay has no upside and a downside when conditions matter): replayed actions now run at 150% bar speed, a gain the rotation cannot give. The replay stops below 50% HP, so heal conditions get control back. Free Hand adds free mana as a pick.

### Rout

`R4-TMP-18` · Fight length and pacing · failed both reviews · appeal 2/5

After 20 landed hits, losing enemies under 50% HP flee and count as kills.

Does: After your 20th landed hit, a living enemy under 50% HP can flee. It flees if it lost a bigger share of HP than you did. A fled enemy counts as a kill.
Costs: A fled enemy pays half XP and half its drop chances.
Grows: The hit count falls 1 per 10 levels, down to 12.
Fails: Bosses, undead and constructs never flee. An enemy that is not losing fights on.

Notes: Share means the percent of max HP lost. Test runs on every landed hit after the count is reached, per enemy. Worked example: an ogre with 400 HP, you with 200 HP. At your 20th hit the ogre is at 45% HP (55% lost) and you are at 80% HP (20% lost). It flees. A fled enemy leaves the fight and does not return. Fights shorter than 20 hits never trigger it. On long fights the fight ends about halfway for half the rewards, so XP per hour stays about flat, damage taken falls, and kill counters (quests, bestiary, kill-based growth) tick faster. Escort spiders flee one by one while the queen, a boss, fights on. Name changed from Judges' Decision because no judge exists. Hard Judges and Quick Judges are cut: Quick ended every fight at 8 hits. Needs a bestiary check that the first floors have living non-boss enemies of 40 or more hits to kill, or the ability rarely fires. Differs from Duel and Bribe (change who fights) and Turncoat (needs low HP and switches sides). Lock test: a boss, an undead or an enemy with the lead on the enemy side never flees.

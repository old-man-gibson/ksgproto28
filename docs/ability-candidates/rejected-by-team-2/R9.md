### Bingo Card

`R9-BRD-07` · Other genres: board and puzzle games · failed both reviews · appeal 2/5

A 3 by 3 card of fight feats. Each marked line gives 4% damage.

Does: A 3 by 3 card holds nine fight feats you have done before. A marked row, column or diagonal gives 4% damage while the card lasts.
Costs: Feats count only against enemies within 3 levels of you. Flasks, dishes and blessings used for a feat are spent.
Grows: A full card adds 1% damage and max HP for the run, up to 20%.
Fails: A card lasts 20 fights, then all marks clear. With fewer than 9 known feats, the card repeats them.
Limit: Count feats rise by 1 on each new card, up to 3 more.

Notes: Data: card: FeatId[9], marks: boolean[9], fightsLeft: number, fullCards: number, known: FeatId[]. A feat joins known the first time it is done against an enemy within 3 levels. A new card is dealt from known only. Placement avoids the same feat twice in one line when it can. Feat pool: crit 3 times in a fight, dodge or parry 5 blows, kill with a spell, kill with a flask, win with a dish active, win with a blessing active, use a technique, win under 30% HP, kill with Cold. Count feats are crit and dodge-or-parry. Their counts rise by 1 per full card, up to +3, so crit 6 and avoid 8 at most. The old timed feat is gone, so no feat grows to impossible and the 20% cap can be reached. Pins are dropped. All lines pay the same 4%, so direction no longer matters. Example: a divine, cooking, smithing climber knows crit, avoid, dish, blessing, technique, win under 30% HP. The card repeats three of them. One repeated feat sits in a row and a column, so one fight of it marks both. Crit and dish and blessing in one row pays 4% damage until the card ends. Early: the card is a list of things you already do. Late: repeated feats and shared squares make a planned build fill two lines at once. Differs from Daily Quest (one growing quota), Saga (four records) and Style Rank (counts systems): lines make one feat count in two places. Banking: marks come only from enemies within 3 levels and pay a flat damage percent, not a burst, so trash marks are not spent on a boss. Offline: cards advance per fight as online. Monster side: none. Cost: save yes for card state unless Facet ability state is already a free bag, no new registry. Test: a feat never done is never on the card, a fight against an enemy 4 levels below marks nothing, a full row pays 4%, 20 fights clear the marks. Risk: still reads as a quest board, so the card and its marks must be on screen in fight.

Plays: passive · Tier: experimental · InfiniteGacha: IG needs: dish, flask, blessing and technique feats. Crit, dodge, kill-by-element and low-HP-win squares work in both games. IG deals only the feats it has. · Reads: crits, parries and dodges, kills by damage type, flask, dish, blessing, spell and technique use, HP at fight end, enemy level

**Second review: fail, appeal 2/5**

Round-1 objections: Objection 1 (half the card is dead for a 3 to 4 system build): answered in letter, squares are dealt from feats already done, but the repeat rule that fills a short list creates the inverse problem below. Objection 2 (reward by direction is arbitrary): answered, every line pays 4% damage. Objection 3 (25% compounding makes feats impossible): answered, count feats rise by 1 per card up to +3, the timed feat is removed, the 20% cap is reachable.

Reading: 'A quest board that pays 4% a line.' Then, on mulling the repeat rule: 'so if the only feat I have ever done is crit three times, the whole card is crits and I fill it every fight, and every new thing I try makes the card harder for the rest of the run.'

1. The repeat rule makes the card's cost fall with the number of feats the player has ever done, and 'known' only grows, so the ability is strongest in the first fights of a run and gets permanently harder every time the player touches a new system.
   Scenario: Fresh run, known = {crit 3 times}. The card is nine crit squares. Fight 2 against an enemy within 3 levels with 3 crits marks all nine squares: 8 lines x 4% = +32% damage for the card as written, plus a full card (+1% damage, +1% max HP; 20 cards to the cap, every card trivially full). The same player then kills with the tutorial spell (known 2), eats a dish before a fight (3), wins one fight under 30% HP by accident (4), fires the floor-2 technique (5). The card is now 5 distinct feats dealt over 9 squares, and the one-off feats (kill with a spell, win under 30% HP) each sit in 2 to 4 lines and never mark again, so most lines die. A nine-feat player must repeat nine different feats inside 20 level-gated fights to earn what the one-feat player earned from three crits. Pigeonhole makes it exact: a card with N known feats needs min(N, 9) distinct performances for the same payout; with 3 known, any 3x3 Latin square has one constant diagonal (Z3 table i+j: anti-diagonal is all 2), so one feat alone completes a line. 'Known' is monotonic per the Data line, so there is no way back once a feat is tried. Also unclear: whether a full card deals a new card at once or only when the 20 fights run out; the two readings differ by a factor of 20 on the run growth.
   Breaks: mull (the user: 'stupid upon reading it and giving it time to mull over'); against the user's stated values of kitchen-sink play and slow compounding growth, which this inverts

Fix: A feat marks one of its squares per fight (each copy needs its own fight), so a narrow card costs as many fights as a wide card costs feats; and deal from feats done in the last 40 fights instead of ever done, so a one-off stops occupying squares for the run. State when a new card is dealt (on full, on expiry, or both).

**First review: fail, appeal 2/5**

Reading: A bingo card of fight feats. The user reads nine squares, three reward kinds by direction, a 20-fight clock, a level gate, a compounding requirement and pins, and files it as a quest board, which the writer's own risk line also says.

1. Six of nine squares are drawn from a pool spanning eight-plus systems, so for the climb's three-or-four-system build half the card is dead and most lines never complete; the ability degrades to the pinned row, once per 20 fights.
   Scenario: A divine, cooking and smithing climber pins crit, dish and blessing in a row and draws 'kill with a spell', 'kill with a flask', 'use a technique' and 'kill with Cold' into four of the six unpinned squares. Every column and both diagonals pass through a dead square. The run's ability is +25% damage for 10 fights out of every 20.
   Breaks: mull (docs/COMPLETABILITY.md section 6a: the player picks about half the core systems)
2. The reward wiring by direction (rows damage, columns defence, diagonals pools) is arbitrary and needs a second read.
   Scenario: The same three feats pay a different boon depending on where the draw placed them; the player cannot reason about it, only read it off the card.
   Breaks: taste/legibility
3. 'The next card needs 25% more of each feat' compounds timed feats to impossibility, so the stated +20% run cap is never reached and the real cap is whichever feat becomes impossible first.
   Scenario: 'Win in under 15 s' becomes under 2.5 s by card 9; 'crit 3 times' becomes 18 by card 8.
   Breaks: arithmetic/polish (bounded, not broken)

Fix: Draw the unpinned squares only from feats this run has already performed at least once, so the card fits the build; one reward per line; make the per-card requirement growth additive.

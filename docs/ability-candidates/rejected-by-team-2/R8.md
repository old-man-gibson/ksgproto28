### Spare Parts

`R8-BAR-08` · The loadout comes alive · failed both reviews · appeal 3/5

Under 50% HP, hits are paid from your three lowest attributes first.

Does: While HP is under 50%, hits are paid from your three lowest attributes first. Each point absorbs 1% of max HP.
Costs: Absorbed points come off those attributes when the fight ends. One point returns per won fight against an enemy at your level or higher.
Grows: Each level adds 1 to every attribute. That adds 3 points to the pool per level.
Fails: An empty pool leaves the three attributes at 0, which count as 1. Condition ticks skip the pool.
Limit: The pool holds at most 200 points.

Notes: Rule check against the objections. The old notes said lopsided builds win. That was backwards, because the pool is the SUM of the three lowest, so higher lows give a bigger pool. A balanced 12/12/12 build is 15 points ahead of 6/7/8 at every level. Level adds the same 3 points to both. Spare Parts pushes the player to train weak attributes. It does not reward dumping them.

Price, using the reviewer's level-30 caster: STR 36, DEX 37, CHA 38 is a 111 point pool. Spending 60 points leaves 17/17/17. Bar speed is 50 x (1 + 0.015 x DEX) per the reviewer's read of derived.ts:196. DEX 37 to 17 takes it from 77.75 to 62.75, which is 19% slower. A fully empty pool puts DEX at 1, which is 50.75 and 35% slower. Winning the 60 points back takes 60 at-level wins. STR and CHA lose what they give too. The cost is no longer one fight of 5%.

Strength check. Under 50% HP the damage budget is 50% plus the pool. Strawman at level 1: 71% against 50%. Strawman at level 30: 158% against 50%, about 3.2 times the last stand. 1% per point is a placeholder. If pacing shows it is too strong, cut it to 0.5% before touching anything else.

Why at-level wins only: the law says a counter must not be bankable on a weak enemy and spent on a boss. Wins against lower-level enemies return 0, so farming trash does not refill the pool. The pool also cannot exceed the stat sheet sum, so nothing can be banked above full.

Data shape: `{ spent: number }`, one saved number per run. Pool = max(0, sum of the three lowest unspent attributes - spent). Points come off the highest of the three first, so the three stay level. The per-attribute loss is derived from `spent` each time. `spent` is clamped to the sum, so a dish buff expiring never makes the pool negative. If training raises another attribute above one of the three, the set changes and the loss moves with it. The loss is a negative add on the stat sheet like the Level N source, so training ranks never see it. `spent` must go in modFingerprint.

Timing: the stat sheet does not change during a fight. The loss and the +1 return land at fight end. That means no derived-stats rebuild per hit and no in-fight stat spiral. The cost lands on the next fights.

Edge cases. A hit is paid from the pool if HP is at or under 50% at the moment of the hit. A big hit at 51% HP is not protected. A hit larger than the pool pays the rest from HP. The cap of 200 points binds near level 60 for 6/7/8 and near level 56 for 12/12/12. It is a safety cap, not a balance cap.

Offline: the same rules run per fight in the 8 h catch-up. A floor where HP stays over 50% never touches the pool. A floor that keeps dropping you under 50% empties it in a few fights and leaves the three attributes at 1. The player then moves to easier at-level fights. This is a setback, not a dead end. Each level-up adds 1 to every attribute, and a prestige run starts with spent at 0. Open question: whether a knockout should clear part of `spent`.

Differences. One Pillar is a static attribute remap. Mana Shield pays from a pool you also use. Weakest Link punishes lopsided builds. Here the shield is made of the stats you use least, and the bill is those stats.

Test that locks it: pool(level L) - pool(level 1) = 3 x (L - 1) below the cap. A hit at 51% HP skips the pool and a hit at 50% pays from it. A win against a lower-level enemy returns 0 and a win against an equal-level enemy returns 1. Condition ticks skip the pool. `spent` never exceeds the sum. No NaN when max HP is 0. Save: one number in the ability state, with no new registry.

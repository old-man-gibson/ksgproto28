### Overchannel

`R2-ARC-18` · Arcane magic · failed both reviews · appeal 3/5

Eight seconds of casting past empty, then you stay dry until it is repaid.

Adds an Overchannel action to the bar, fired by the triggers you set (such as enemy HP over 50%). For 8 seconds a spell is castable while mana minus its cost stays above a floor of minus twice your max mana, and spell cooldowns are halved, so the pool does not stop you. Mana keeps regenerating as normal, and you cannot cast any spell until mana is back above that spell's cost, so the dry time equals what you cast on credit. The window grows by 1 s per 10 Arcana levels and the floor rises with your max mana.

Notes: Example: Arcana 30, max mana 120, regen 3/s, four spells at 30 mana with 6 s cooldown. Fire at full mana: floor -240, window 11 s, cooldowns 3 s, about 12 casts in the window (what normal casting takes 120 s to fund). Mana ends near -210 and takes about 70 s to reach 0 and 10 s more to reach 30 for the next cast. A boss that outlasts the window finds you dry. Early run: 30-mana pool, 1/s regen, 5-mana spells: floor -60, about 12 casts, 60 s of dry afterward; same ratio. Triggers read mana as max(0, mana) / max, so no negative percentages reach a comparison. Differs from Promissory Flesh (a damage ledger due at dawn): this is a mana balance that regen repays in seconds, with no ledger to read. The trigger is the decision and the sim reads it, so offline needs no input. Dry time is the old hangover, now proportional to use, with no fixed 16 s.

Plays: rotation action · Reads: mana and max mana, regen rate, spell mana costs and cooldowns, rotation triggers (enemy HP, mana as max(0, mana) over max), Arcana level

### Over the Wall

`R2-ARC-11` · Arcane magic · failed both reviews · appeal 2/5

Spells skip the front line and hit the enemy mage first.

Your spells go to the enemy with the highest INT instead of the front one; weapon blows still hit the front, and a tie or a lone enemy falls back to the front. Every spell that lands adds to Reach, a tally kept for the whole run: 1 if it hit an enemy other than the front one, 0.25 if it hit the front. Every spell cuts its target's resistance by 0.5 x Reach / (Reach + 4000), so what you earn on back-row casters also cuts into a lone boss (about 0.07 after an hour of casting, 0.23 after five). The price is that every spell cools down 15% slower, and while a higher-INT enemy lives the front enemy takes no spell damage and keeps hitting you. Floors with no back row grow Reach at a quarter rate, so the tax is paid in full and the gain is slow.

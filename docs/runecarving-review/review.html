Runecarving

Runes made of glyphs. A rune is the sum of its parts, read differently by the slot it sits in, and able to rewrite one technique card without writing a chain. Findings in today's code, the team's merged design, the objections raised against it, and six working mockups. Glyph art: Idylwild's Runic Codex (CC0).

Verdict

team: 3 brainstormers, 1 design antagonist
TodayFour stat-bundle patterns, ten adornments, four exact-recipe named runes. Combinations are many but the outcomes are few: every carving is a scaled sum of about fourteen stat lines. Runes are behind the alpha shutter and their stones have no source. Carved runes cannot touch techniques. Six bugs, four of them breaking "same inputs, same rune".
ProposedA rune is a set of glyphs from the codex, in any order. Each glyph adds points to a few of thirteen aspects (Power, Guard, Health, Speed, Crit, Magic, Luck, and the six elements). The rune's totals are the sum. Each slot turns the totals into different stats, so the same rune does different things in a weapon, a helmet or a ring. 4 glyph points in one aspect turn on a bonus; a higher total in one aspect turns on a named rune. The bench rolls no dice.
Identity"The sum of its elements", as you put it. Nothing reads order (alchemy), adjacency or pairs (mutagens), proportion or tallies of verbs (smithing), time (cooking) or a classifier (music). Every bonus reads one aspect. Today's named runes are the one exception; question 1 asks whether to keep it.
EquipmentSockets become deterministic by size (1 / 2 / 3). Rune value reads its inputs. The slot decides the stats: three Fair Algiz runes in a vest take the floor-3 boss duel from 0/20 to 14–18/20 at John's band-one end state (two probes, two kits); the same runes on the club give 0/20.
MartialRunes select technique cards by printed properties (event, kind, entry, exit), never by id. Threshold bonuses cut cooldowns, add penetration, and make hits inflict an element's status. Some glyphs add a fork option to a technique's Honed choice. Named runes rewrite one specific technique. No rune chains techniques together; the planner shows what changed.
Core systemYes for sustain in band one, once the unlock sits on floor 2 (measured twice). Damage supporting. Condition defence through element resistances. Access unmeasured.
AntagonistFix first: 13 objections. 12 accepted and folded in below, 1 sent to you. No terminal state, no gold loop, no edge list found.
SizeSix slices. Slice 1 is bug fixes with no design question. Slices 2–6 need the alpha freeze lifted for runecarving (question 2).

The merged design

first-guess numbers
GlyphsCodex runes, each a small vector over 13 aspects and a stroke count.
→
SlabA multiset of glyphs on a graded stone, plus adornments. No order, no dice.
→
TotalsGlyph points × stone grade, plus adornments. A bonus at 4 points in one aspect; a named rune at a higher total.
→
SocketEach slot turns the totals into its own stats: weapon, each armor slot, accessory. Larger runes take more sockets.
→
TechniquesBonuses, added fork options and named runes change matching techniques, one technique at a time.

The carving

Aspects
Power, Guard, Health, Speed, Crit, Magic, Luck, and six elements: Heat, Cold, Lightning, Poison, Light, Dark.
Glyphs
38 authored rows: the 24 Elder Futhark and 14 Anglo-Saxon additions (these supply the elements the Futhark is thin on: Ior storm, Þorn venom, Éar gloam). Each is 3 points (primary 2, secondary 1) chosen from its meaning, plus strokes from the drawn art. Variants (short-twig, long-branch) carry the parent's vector at half the strokes. Letter runes carry 1 point of the parent's primary at 1 stroke. Calendar marks stay unused (time is cooking's).
Totals
Σ glyph points × stone grade + Σ adornment assays × each one's own grade. Adornments are one aspect each: gels are elements, gold leaf Luck, bone dust Health, ochre Power, verdigris Poison, garnet Crit, sapphire Magic.
Readings
One linear line per aspect per context (weapon, armor, accessory). Armor reads through a slot lens: chest ×1; head ×0.5 but Magic and Crit ×1.5; hands ×0.35 but Power and Speed ×1; feet ×0.35 but Speed ×1.5; legs ×0.7; offhand reads Guard as parry.
Bonuses
4 glyph points in one aspect, in one rune, turn on that aspect's bonus: Speed (reflex cooldown ×0.85 / dodge +3pp), Guard (crit damage taken −10%), Crit (strike penetration +0.1). Each element's bonus makes weapon hits inflict its status (Cold chilled, Heat on fire, Lightning jolted, Poison poison, Light dazzled, Dark blinded; 20%). Each bonus reads one aspect only.
Named runes
Recommended: a named rune is a bonus with a name, on one aspect, at a higher total (6–8 after grade; some need a minimum slab grade). Adding a glyph never turns one off; all active named runes apply. Unknown ones show "?" on the chart until made once or taught. Named runes that need several aspects (today's shape) read two aspects together and break the guard: your ruling (question 1).
Bench
No split chance, no upgrade chance. F slabs and F adornments refused. Cut slots open at Runecarving 0 / 5 / 10 / 18 / 25 / 35.
Sockets used
Strokes ÷ strokes per socket, rounded up. Strokes per socket: stone grade (E 4, D 6, C 8, B 11, A 15, S 20) + tool + Runecarving level ÷ 10. A rune that needs more than 3 sockets (the largest item) is refused at the bench. Better tools and skill fit more glyphs into one socket; they change no odds. A burin is needed for variants and letters.
Value
Half the sale value of the inputs at their grades. Glyphs add nothing.

Equipment

Sockets
Small 1, medium 2, large 3. Legacy items keep their rolls. Item value reads the class constant, never the runes in it.
Swap
Unsocketing is free and returns the whole rune, so one rune can move between slots as needed.
Supply
Data only: blank runestone (Fair, pinned) 24 gold at a shop counter and 10% from the floor-2 automatons; ochre, verdigris and a bronze chisel as shop lines; a steel burin line after floor 2; bone dust from the living doll; garnet from a floor-3 find; sapphire on the floor-3 boss's first clear. The unlock find sits on floor 2 (the break room), below the boss it helps with.
Knowledge
Four starter glyphs: Algiz, Uruz, Isaz, Fehu. A monster that bears a rune teaches its glyphs after five kills (a bestiary fact). Codex pages from finds teach the rest.
Presets
The eleven catalogue runes become fixed carvings that loot mints. Gale, Earthbind and Umbral become named runes that keep their technique changes. Save v58 rewrites catalogue ids already socketed in items to their preset carvings, so nothing goes inert.
Monsters
MonsterDef.runes per slot, through the same pure readers, all 13 aspects (Speed attack speed, Crit crit, Magic spell power included). The bestiary prints the exact carving.

Martial

Selector
Printed-card properties: event, kind (strike, buff, counter), entry, exit, family. Never a technique id. Read on the printed card, so one pass and no order.
Reach
Weapon runes reach strikes. Armor, offhand and accessory runes reach buffs, counters and the onHit / onParry / onDodge answers. A buckler card belongs to the offhand, not the weapon.
Tunes
Bonuses cut cooldown and add penetration on matching techniques; element bonuses add their status to every weapon hit, technique strikes included. Summed, then clamped (cooldown −40%, penetration +0.3).
Fork options
Some glyphs add an existing fork option (weight 0.7 × grade per copy). A total of 1.0 adds that option to the matching card's Honed offering while socketed, at tier III, before a path is taken. Taking it is permanent, like every Honed choice. Void if its exit is the card's own entry.
Named runes
A named rune in the weapon may rewrite one specific technique (Gale: Sidestep; Earthbind: Thrust's exit; Umbral: Riposte). Which change wins for a technique's exit and name: named rune, then the chosen fork, then the printed card; between named runes, registry order. One rewrite per technique.
Planner
A badge on every rune-rewritten edge, naming the runes; a ghost line to the printed exit. Socketing refreshes learning at once.

Objections and rulings

antagonist verdict: fix first

No terminal state, no gold loop, no edge list, no non-finite value found. The totals are a pure sum and the caps in derived.ts bound every reading. Thirteen objections: twelve accepted and already in the design above, one sent to you. One more ruling came from the mockups.

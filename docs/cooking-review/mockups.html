Mockups for docs/COOKING_REVIEW.md. Proposed UI on today's stove (alpha: whole food, two slots below Cooking 8). Not game code.

A · Heat track and dish windows (review §4.1, §5.1)

Each slot is a lane on the time axis. The bar under an ingredient runs from its entry unit to the end of the cook: hatched is raw, filled is inside its band, red hatch is burnt. The strip under the meat lane shows the stew's dish windows and where the meat lands; changing the duration or the meat's slot changes the dish. Today the stove shows none of this: the band only appears in a filled cell's tooltip, and the slots sit in a grid out of time order.

B · Grade ledger (review §4.3, §5.2)

The preview opens to show why the dish lands where it does. Struck-through numbers are today's formula; the proposal caps the cook's judgement at +2.00, so a Divine dish needs Superb ingredients. Today the preview is one line, and its grade colour is set but never drawn (BenchPanel.svelte:645 sets --tint; .preview never reads it).

C · Plate card at completion (review §5.3)

Shown at the bench when a cook finishes, instead of only a log line. Grade stamp, the roll against the range, and the dish's count and best grade from the Cook's Book.

D · Cook's Book (review §4.4, §5.3)

The re-openable reference Invariant B asks for. One row per dish: a square per grade (filled = made at that grade, dashed = next grade up from your best), count, the window once pinned (25 cooks), Signature at 100. Dishes not yet made show as a silhouette with no gate printed.

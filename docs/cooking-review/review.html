Summary

team: 3 brainstormers, 1 design antagonist
IdentityCooking is about timing. Today cook time only changes a dish's grade. After round 1 it also decides which dish you get. In round 3 each animal part and each jelly has its own cook time. Cooking does not get pair bonuses (mutagens), order rules (alchemy) or added-up effects (runecarving).
GoldCooking may earn gold (your answer 1). Today, at Cooking 45, 10 gold of Fair (C) ingredients make a Divine (S) stew that sells for 56. The round 1 skill cap lowers that to Superb (A, 35 gold), so better ingredients still matter at high levels.
Round 1Building now. Skill bonus capped at +2.00. Heat windows: the same ingredients make Seared Stew, Hearty Stew or Braised Meat depending on cook time. Steeped Broth for poison resistance. A condition does not land at 100% resistance. Stove screen fixes, the heat track and the grade ledger.
Round 2Next. Food lasts longer and works better as Cooking levels up. Cook counts per dish, Signature dishes at 100 cooks, the Cook's Book, the plate card, and a prestige boon that keeps part of the Book.
HuntingRound 3. Slimes drop jelly, one flavor per slime family. Animals drop parts. Humanoids are not food unless the player has an omnivore or cannibal flag.

The plan

numbers are first guesses

Kitchen

Heat windows
A dish can require that an ingredient cooks for a set time. Without the recipe card, or outside that time, you get the same dish as today.
Round 1 dishes
Seared Stew: meat and a vegetable, meat cooks under 6 units. Gives Stoked: +5% damage and +5% crit damage, 5 minutes. Braised Meat: meat cooks 14 units or more. Gives Well Fed ×1.5 for 15 minutes. Steeped Broth: mushroom and herb, herb cooks 8 units or more. Gives Steeped: +25% poison and +10% acid resistance, 10 minutes. Hearty Stew and Mushroom Broth stay as they are.
Grade
Every status number above is multiplied by the dish's grade: Fair (C) ×1, Fine (B) ×1.6, Superb (A) ×2.5, Divine (S) ×4.
How to make them
At Cooking 1 with two slots. Seared: meat in the midway slot on a Quick simmer (5.4 units). Braised: meat first on a Long braise (16 units). The recipe cards at the Larder Door say the same.
Skill bonus
Cooking level ÷ 15 up to level 15, then +1 per 45 levels, at most +2.00 (level 60). Fair (C) ingredients can reach Superb (A). Divine (S) needs Superb (A) ingredients.
Immunity
A condition does not land if the target's resistance to it, minus the attacker's penetration, is 100% or more. Below 100% nothing changes. A status can also grant immunity to an element or a condition. Monsters follow the same rule.
Levels
Round 2. Every 10 Cooking levels, food lasts 6% longer. Every 20, food is 5% stronger. Both stop at ×1.5.
Signatures
Round 2. After 25 cooks of a dish, the Cook's Book shows its cook time. After 100, it is a Signature and also gives a second, smaller status.
Cook's Book
Round 2. One row per dish: grades made, count, best grade, cook time, Signature. Dishes you have not made are blank rows. Resets on prestige; a boon keeps part of it.
Crocks
After base building. Jars that pickle while you do other things. Collecting early or late lowers the grade. Needs a save version change.

Hunting

Slime jelly
Round 3. Each slime family drops its own jelly: hot, cold, spicy, tingling, kingly, and so on. Higher level and tier slimes drop better grades. Each jelly has its own cook time.
Animal parts
Round 3. Living beasts drop parts based on their tags: tender cut, sinew, offal; large ones also drop marrow. Cook times: tender cut 3–9 units, sinew 12–16, offal 2–5, marrow 10–16.
Glands
Round 3. A species with 25% or more resistance to an element drops a gland. Gland and herb make a broth that resists that element.
What is food
Humanoids, goblins included, are not food. An ability, gene or mutagen can give the player an omnivore or cannibal flag that changes this.
Palate
Round 3. A new bestiary entry, learned after at least 4 kills. Lists the species' parts, their cook times and glands. A Load Pot button fills the stove so every part cooks the right time.
Bait
Round 4. Each species eats certain foods, set by its tags. A bait dish makes those species show up more often, up to ×3, more with higher Hunting. At Hunting 40, some rare monsters only come to bait (optional content).
Feeding
Round 4. Give the front enemy a dish it eats and it stops attacking until it is hit or a timer runs out. Optional content only.
Dressing
Later. How the monster died changes its parts' grade: bleeding +1, poisoned −2.

Objections and rulings

antagonist verdict: fix first

No way to get stuck: no required path uses parts or heat windows, every food source refills, and prestige re-earns everything. Seven objections, all accepted. The plan above includes each ruling.

SeverityObjectionRuling
highThe first version of the skill cap was not a cap. Fair ingredients still reached Divine, at Cooking 105 instead of 45. Cooking has no level limit.Accepted: capped at +2.00. Your answer 1 then allowed cooking to earn gold; the cap stays so ingredients matter.
highHeat windows could make the normal stew worse. Recipe checks run before ingredient checks, so a long braise without the Braised card fell through to Traveler's Ration instead of Hearty Stew.Accepted. The dish without a cook-time rule stays the default. A check makes sure every layout still makes at least what it makes today.
highAnimal parts claimed three roles; only one had a source. No live animal is large (no marrow) or 25% resistant (no glands).Accepted. Moved to round 3. Your answer 3 (slimes are food) gives it a source.

mediumSteeped Broth was too weak to count. −8% poison at Fair.Accepted. Your answer 2 set it to +25% per grade multiplier, with immunity at 100%.
lowThe gland broth's status was called ward_*, which is already an arcane magic word and a stat.Accepted. Renamed when built.
lowA cooking XP-for-new-dishes system needs a save migration, or old saves get a one-time XP bonus per dish.Accepted. It ships in round 2 with its migration.

Problems with today's screen

  1. The slots are not in time order. The midway slot is above the first one; slots 3 and 4 are in the corners.
  2. The right cook time for an ingredient only shows in its tooltip after you place it.
  3. The dish preview is one line. It does not say why, and its grade color is never drawn.
  4. Cookware's grade counts like an ingredient's, and the screen does not say so.
  5. The picker shows every grade of an onion with the same icon. During the review an F-grade onion went in by accident.
  6. Reopening the stove during a cook shows an empty pot next to the progress bar.

Proposed

Each slot is a row on a time line. The bar under an ingredient starts when it goes in and ends when the cook ends. Striped: not cooked enough yet. Solid: cooked the right amount. Red stripes: overcooked. The bottom row shows which dish each meat cook time makes, and where the meat is now. Change the cook time or the meat's slot to see the dish change.

4 · The Cook's Book

round 2

Every dish in one list. Filled square: you made it at that grade. Dashed square: the next grade up from your best. Then how many you cooked, the cook time once you have made it 25 times, and the Signature at 100. Dishes you have not made are blank rows. Example save.

6 · Bait

round 4

A bait dish makes species that eat it show up more often: × (1.5 + Hunting ÷ 40), at most ×3. Example: the floor 1 storerooms spawn rats 75% of the time and slimes 25%. Slimes eat sweet food; rats do not.

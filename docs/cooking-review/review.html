Cooking and Hunting

How to make cooking distinct and worth mastering while it keeps its identity, how hunting can feed it, and what the stove should look like. Findings from the code, the team's plan in rounds, the objections raised against it, and six working mockups.

Verdict

team: 3 brainstormers, 1 design antagonist
IdentityCooking stays when. Today timing decides how good a dish is. The plan makes timing also decide which dish it is. Later, each part of an animal arrives with its own cook time, so the quarry tells the cook when it goes in. No pair synergies (mutagens), no order rules (alchemy), no summed effects (runecarving).
Fix firstThe stove mints gold. At Cooking 45, Fair Larder ingredients worth about 10 gold make a Divine stew that sells for 56, every 12 seconds, and bench runs keep going offline: about 110k gold a night against about 1k held at the end of band 1. A cap on the cook's skill bonus fixes the worst of it.
Round 1Cap the skill bonus at +2.00; heat windows (same pot, different cook time, different dish); the stove UI fixes; a heat track and a grade ledger on the stove. No save version change.
HuntingDesigned, held back. The tower spawns slimes and constructs; the only animals that drop meat live on the shelved maps. Carcass parts and the Palate fact wait for living quarry on a live floor, or for your answer on whether slimes are food.
RolesBasic cooking would fill sustain (stews), damage (Seared Stew), and maybe condition defence (Steeped Broth, unproven). Access only in optional content.

The plan

first-guess numbers

The kitchen

Heat windows
A stove outcome may declare windows on a tag. It matches when the mean time on the heat of the parts carrying that tag falls inside. The window-less dish stays the fallback for its ingredients, so a player without the card, or outside every window, cooks exactly what they cook today.
First set
Meat + vegetable: Seared Stew when meat cooks under 6 units (grants Stoked: +5% damage and +5% crit damage per intensity, 5 min); Hearty Stew otherwise (Well Fed); Braised Meat at 14 or more (Well Fed ×1.5, 15 min). Mushroom + herb: Steeped Broth when the herb cooks 8 or more (poison and acid resistance); Mushroom Broth otherwise.
Reachable
At Cooking 1, two slots, unprepped food. Seared: meat midway at Quick simmer (5.4 units). Braised: meat first at Long braise (16). Steady stew with meat first stays Hearty (12).
Judgement cap
min(L, 15) ÷ 15, then + (L − 15) ÷ 45, capped at +2.00 (reached at 60). Fair inputs top out at Superb; Divine needs Superb ingredients. Levels 15–60 still pay, 0.022 each.
Milestones
Round 2. Every 10 levels food lasts 6% longer; every 20, 5% stronger. Both capped at ×1.5, because cooking has no level cap.
Signatures
Round 2. 25 cooks of a dish pins its window in the Cook's Book. 100 makes it a Signature: eating it also grants a second, smaller status.
Cook's Book
Round 2. One row per dish: grades made, count, best, window, Signature. Unmade dishes show as a blank row with no gate printed.
Crocks
After base building. A bench on the world clock: start a pickle, collect later; the grade reads time at rest with the stove's own fit curve. A save change.

The hunt

Carcass parts
Held. A kill rolls parts from the monster's tags instead of a per-monster meat line: tender cut, sinew, offal for living beasts; marrow for large ones. Zero authoring per monster; a monster may override.
Parts' windows
Each part has its own cook band: tender cut 3–9, sinew 12–16, offal 2–5, marrow 10–16. The animal decides which slot each part belongs in.
Glands
A species with innate elemental resistance of 0.25 or more drops a gland; gland + herb makes a broth that resists that element.
Palate
A new bestiary fact (4 kills minimum): the species' parts, their windows, its glands, and a Load Pot button that fills the stove with a layout that lands every part in band.
Bait
Later. Each species has a diet from its tags. A bait dish multiplies the encounter weight of matching species, up to ×3, growing with Hunting. At Hunting 40, rare quarry answers only bait (optional content).
Offering
Later. Feeding the front enemy a dish it eats makes it Sated: it stops attacking until hit or until a timer runs out. Optional content only.
Dressing
Later, low priority. How it died shifts its parts' grade: bleeding +1, poisoned −2.

Objections and rulings

antagonist verdict: fix first

No state the player cannot leave: no critical path touches parts or windows, every food source is endless, prestige re-earns per run, and the identity guards hold. Seven objections, all accepted. The plan above includes each ruling.

Questions for you

answer in fragments
  1. Is cooking a gold source? After the cap, Fair inputs still make Superb stews worth 3.5× their cost, and bench runs replay offline. Default: the cap alone. Alternative: dish resale stops scaling with grade past Fair, so a dish's worth is eating it.
  2. How strong may a cooking status be against poison? (a) A resistance a test passes, about +0.25 at Fair. (b) Poison cannot land at all while Steeped is held (monsters can wear it too). (c) Cooking does not claim condition defence. Default: (a), tested.
  3. Are slimes food? 37 of 64 species are slimes, and they are the tower's main quarry. Yes makes slime jelly the alpha's hunt-to-pot link. Default: no until you say; the hunt half waits for living quarry.
  4. Are goblins food? Default: no, humanoids are excluded.
  5. Does the Cook's Book survive prestige? Default: it resets per run like recipe cards; a prestige boon may carry it later.
  6. Is a 100-cook Signature grind wanted? Default: yes; the numbers are data.
  7. Round 1 scope. Default: as in the table above.

What it hides

  1. Slots sit out of time order: the midway slot is above the first one, slots 3 and 4 are in the corners.
  2. The heat band appears only in a filled cell's tooltip, so you cannot see where a part should go before placing it.
  3. The dish preview is one line, with no reason and no grade colour (it sets one; the style never reads it).
  4. Cookware's grade is averaged in like an ingredient, and nothing says so.
  5. The picker shows every grade of an onion as the same icon. The review run put a Refuse onion in by accident.
  6. Reopening the stove mid-cook shows an empty pot beside a running bar.

Proposed

Each slot is a lane on the time axis. The bar under a part runs from its entry to the end of the cook: hatched is raw, filled is inside its band, red hatch is burnt. The strip under the lanes shows the stew's dish windows and where the meat lands. Change the cook time or the meat's slot and watch the dish change.

2 · The grade ledger

round 1 · preview

The dish preview opens to show why the dish lands where it does. Live from mockup 1. Struck-through numbers are today's formula; the plan caps the cook's judgement at +2.00, so at high levels only better ingredients close the gap. In the game it is collapsed to one line until opened.

3 · The plate card

round 2 · completion

Shown at the bench when a cook finishes, instead of only a log line: grade stamp in the grade's colour, the score, and the dish's count and best grade from the Cook's Book. Live from mockup 1.

4 · The Cook's Book

round 2 · reference

The re-openable reference the completability law asks for. A square per grade (filled: made at that grade; dashed: the next grade up from your best), count, the window once pinned, the Signature once earned. Dishes not yet made show as a blank row with no gate printed, so the stove keeps its discovery. Example save.

6 · Bait: the kitchen feeds the hunt

round 4 · encounter weights

A bait dish raises the encounter weight of species whose diet matches, by 1 + 0.5 + Hunting ÷ 40, capped at ×3. Example: the floor-1 storerooms roll rats 75 and slimes 25. Slimes eat sweet things; rats do not.

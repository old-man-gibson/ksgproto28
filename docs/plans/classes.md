# Classes

> Seed recorded 2026-08-05. **Unshaped — not designed yet.** Full seed text in
> [NEXT.md](../NEXT.md) §"Parked: four subsystem seeds".

Exclusive like titles — one at a time; **prestige systems may open gestalt
tracks**. Classes level and reshape the playstyle:

- **barbarian** — a rage resource bar that fills on taking damage.
- **rogue** — leans on flat-footed/surprised for bonus precision damage.
  Surprised's new teeth (`incomingBite`/`opensTempo`) are exactly this class's
  food.
- **wizard** — limits the technique pool in exchange for arcane bonuses and
  learning to **write scrolls**.

**Genuinely new:** event-filled resource bars (rage fills where `damageTaken`
fires — a small chassis over the existing bus), and formal grant-plus-constraint
class definitions.

## ⚠️ Settle first

**What staying classless means.** The game's identity is breadth, so a class
must be an opt-in *sharpening*, never a mandatory funnel. This is the seed most
likely to violate [COMPLETABILITY.md](../COMPLETABILITY.md) by default.

Natural piecemeal-unlock candidate.

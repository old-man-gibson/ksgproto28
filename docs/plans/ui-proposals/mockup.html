3. Inventory with 1,586 items

C2 spare marks and compare to worn

  • Spare: another item you hold of the same base is at least as good in every stat and rune slot.
  • Items with conditional stats, forged items, festival weapons and loadout items are never marked.
  • Sell spares uses the existing sell price. Socketed runes are kept, as today.
  • The example pack is four Leather Vest stacks. The vest with 4 slots and Sturdy beats the plain and 2-slot Sturdy vests.
